Questions we hear first.

Who are you?

A small team of engineers across mobile, the API and the dashboard. We build the app, the API and the dashboard together, so nothing gets lost between teams.

What does the audit cost?

A Focus audit (one layer) is $1,200 and a Full audit (all three layers) is $2,400. The first ten audits are at launch prices of $900 and $1,900. You pay upfront, and the whole amount is credited to your first project if you sign within 30 days.

Where do you work?

Remotely, with clients anywhere in the world. We work in English or Arabic and overlap with your working hours.

Can I see your code?

The case studies show what we built and which part was ours. Under an NDA we can walk you through the code itself.

Who owns the code?

You do. Ownership passes to you at delivery and is written into the contract.
